Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

ABReader is an eBook reader that runs in your browser (https://reader.abdell.dev) and as a desktop app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes and how you can delete it.

Your data stays on your device

Your books, reading progress, highlights, notes, vocabulary, statistics and settings are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage) or, in the desktop app, on your computer.

ABReader has no server of its own and no user accounts. We never receive a copy of your books or of anything you write in the app.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, ABReader asks only for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. With it the app can see and change only the files it created itself or the files you pick with the Google file picker. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

ABReader uses this access to: back up and sync your library and reading data to your own Drive; store and download text-to-speech audio you choose to keep; and import books you select from Drive.

The sign-in token is kept in your browser session and is sent only to Google. The data read from or written to Drive travels directly between your browser and Google; it never passes through any server of ours.

ABReader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

You can disconnect Drive from the app's settings at any time, and revoke ABReader's access from your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Usage statistics

The web version uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics may set cookies and collects technical data such as pages visited, approximate location, browser and device type. It never receives the content of your books, your notes or your Google Drive data.

The app reports a generic page title to Google Analytics, but while a book is open the browser tab title includes that book's title, and some automatic Analytics events may still send it.

Optional online features

Some features send data to other services only when you use them:

Dictionary and translation: the word you look up is sent to dictionaryapi.dev, Wiktionary (wiktionary.org), Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) and MyMemory.

Downloads from other addresses: when you import a book or a voice from a URL, or a book refers to images hosted elsewhere (for example its cover), the app downloads them from those servers, which see your IP address.

Icons: some interface icons may be loaded from api.iconify.design.

Azure voices: if you enter your own Microsoft Azure key, the text being read aloud is sent to Microsoft Azure to generate the audio.

Browser and system voices: some voices offered by your browser or operating system (for example the "natural" voices in Microsoft Edge) are generated online by that provider.

Edge Voice Lab: text is sent only to the address you configure.

Local voices: voice models and the runtime that plays them are downloaded from huggingface.co and cdn.jsdelivr.net. After that, the text never leaves your device.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Apart from the services listed above, which you use directly from your browser, no one else receives data from ABReader.

Deleting your data

To delete your library and reading data, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Clear All Data. To remove absolutely everything the app stored, including downloaded voice models, clear this site's data in your browser.

Copies saved in your Google Drive are yours: you can delete them from Drive, and revoke the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

ABReader is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to info@abdell.dev.
